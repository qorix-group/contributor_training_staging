title: Continuous improvement: what should be better in this training
labels: improvement
---
This issue stays open for the whole week, and it may stay open afterwards. Whenever something in the training is unclear, wrong, broken or could be better, add a comment here right away:

- **Bug**: something does not work as the task says (a command fails, a link is dead, a branch does not build as described).
- **Finding**: something in the documentation, the process or the tooling that you found questionable, even if it is not part of a task.
- **Improvement**: something that would have made the task clearer, faster or more realistic.

One comment per point. Say where (issue, file, step) and what you expected. Your copy is yours alone: what should reach the maintainers, send as **Feedback** or **Bug report** to `eclipse-score/contributor_training`, as `TRAINING.md` describes. Do not wait for the end of the week: what you remember on day 5 is less than what you noticed on day 1.

**Done when** your retrospective in task 6.2-6.3 summarises these comments on your participant page. Close this issue with that pull request, or leave it open for further comments; the certificate does not check it.
