title: 6.2-6.3 Formal review of your document, and your retrospective
labels: stage-6
---
1. Perform the formal document review (FDR) of your document from task 5.4 with the checklist the module provides for it. Record the filled-in checklist in the repository.
2. Add a retrospective to your participant page. Summarise your comments on the continuous improvement issue. What was missing in this training? Which S-CORE issue would you take on first? The S-CORE User's Guide, "What's Next?", says where to find good first issues.

**Done when** one merged pull request contains both. It closes this issue and any task or finding issue still open; the continuous improvement issue may stay open.

Afterwards, issue your certificate of completion and tell the training that you finished, as `TRAINING.md` describes. Send your summary of the continuous improvement comments as **Feedback** to `eclipse-score/contributor_training`: your copy is yours alone, so nobody else reads it there.
